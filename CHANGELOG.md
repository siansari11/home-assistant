# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Tasks screen matches the Status Board layout: stat tiles, Do now cards, and a deadline timeline.
- Home deadlines use the same overdue and upcoming colors.
- Household sync accepts `HOUSEHOLD_KEY_HASH` (sha-256 hex) and still accepts `HOUSEHOLD_KEY`.

## 1.1.0

- Kitchen timer still rings until Dismiss.
- Tasks screen for personal deadlines, with a My deadlines card on Home.
- Household sync for chores, meals, shopping, the sticky note, the house checklist, and tasks.
- Todoist two-way sync through the `our-home-sync` worker.
- Android rotates in portrait and landscape.
- Removed the other-room alarm link, Spotify connect, and the Alexa skill UI.

## 1.0.0

- First kitchen tablet build. Loads the published Our Home site, keeps the screen on, and installs without the Play Store.
